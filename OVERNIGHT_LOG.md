## Milestone 1 — Deterministic engine core
**Commit `3051356`**

Built the simulation spine: seeded multi-stream RNG (splitmix32), tile world
with procedural rooms/MST-corridor generation and reachability validation,
A* + DDA LOS, player combat/dodge/stamina, enemy AI (perception, memory,
melee/ranged/charger behaviors), NPCs, quests, inventory/equipment/relics,
XP progression, shops/chests/shrines/key+boss gating, checksummed saves.

**Bugs found by tests, fixed at root:**
- `map.rooms` never assigned after generation (LOS test caught it)
- entity separation could push entities into walls → revert-on-invalid pass
- `JSON.stringify(Infinity)` → `null` wiped every TTL on restore — all 14
  entities including the player vanished one tick after load. Fixed with
  sentinel encoding (`__INF__`) + two-pass serialization design.
- restored RNG streams were undefined (class-field initializers never ran)
- `timeSec` rounding desynced AI timers after restore

## Milestone 2 — QA that actually plays
**Commit `c72e776`**

15-tick cadence; portal/key/NPC reachability checks; save/load determinism
